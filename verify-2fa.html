Two-Factor Authentication

Enter the 6-digit code from one of your verified authenticator apps.

If you set up a backup authenticator on another device, select it above and enter its code.

Native Supabase recovery codes are not enabled for this hosted Auth project. If you have lost access to every verified authenticator, contact your school administrator or Smart Learning DC support for an independent identity review. No factor is removed automatically; email or an existing browser session alone is not sufficient proof of identity.

Never share your password, authenticator code, QR code, or setup key with anyone.
