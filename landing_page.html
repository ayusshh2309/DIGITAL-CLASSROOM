Questions, answered

Is it free?

Early access is free. Pricing for schools and tutors will be announced before launch.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Students can join classes and submit work from any phone or laptop browser.

Can I use it for one-to-one tuition?

Yes. A class can have one student or hundreds.

Is student data safe?

Only teachers and enrolled students can see a class and its materials.
